## Rust runtime and compiler support

Rust standard library/compiler support carries its upstream licenses, principally MIT/Apache-2.0 and applicable exceptions. Included `RUST-COPYRIGHT-library.html` preserves the toolchain's standard-library notices; `THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.txt` also includes its supplied license texts.
